Curious about me?

I am a geospatial specialist with a background in architecture, urban strategy, mobility analysis, and earth observation. My work focuses on translating spatial and environmental data into strategies that support climate resilience, sustainable infrastructure, and stronger urban decisions.

Across projects and roles, I use geospatial simulations, analytical workflows, and systems thinking to help cities and institutions optimize infrastructure, understand risk, and make the green and digital transition more actionable.
