Curious about me?

I am a geospatial specialist and product engineer with a background in architecture, urban strategy, mobility analysis, and earth observation. My work focuses on translating spatial, regulatory and environmental data into products and development strategies that support climate resilience, sustainable infrastructure and stronger product decisions.

Across projects and roles, I use geospatial simulations, analytical workflows, and systems thinking to help cities and institutions optimize infrastructure, understand risk, and make the green and digital transition more actionable.

What motivates me is the bridge between technical analysis and implementation: turning complex datasets into frameworks that are both rigorous and useful for product development and decision-making.
