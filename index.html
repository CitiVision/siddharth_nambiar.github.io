What I bring to spatial and urban strategy work.

The original homepage template used a service grid. Here it becomes a sharper overview of the capabilities that define this portfolio: geospatial analysis, urban systems thinking, and simulation-driven decision support.

01 / Geospatial analysis

Spatial evidence that holds up.

GIS, remote sensing, and earth observation workflows that turn territorial complexity into legible insights for planning, infrastructure, and environmental strategy.

  • QGIS / ArcGIS workflows for urban and environmental datasets
  • Google Earth Engine and satellite imagery interpretation
  • Risk metrics, environmental patterning, and spatial decision framing
02 / Urban systems

Strategy across the built environment.

Bringing together architecture, urban systems, mobility strategy, and climate resilience to shape decisions that work at multiple scales.

  • Mobility, emissions, and infrastructure analysis for cities
  • Climate resilience and sustainable infrastructure positioning
  • Stakeholder translation from technical data to planning action
03 / Simulations and systems

Scenario thinking for urban futures.

Building analytical workflows and simulation logic that test interventions, reveal impacts, and support more confident spatial decisions.

  • Agent-based simulations and environmental scenario testing
  • Python, SQL, and computational workflows for scalable analysis
  • Product thinking for decision-support tools and urban platforms
